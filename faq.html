Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your day on the water. Can't find your answer? Message us and we'll reply, usually within the hour.

How do I book a yacht?

Send an enquiry through the form on our homepage or message us on WhatsApp with your date, number of guests and the kind of day you have in mind. We reply with a short list of available yachts and clear pricing, usually within the hour. Your yacht is reserved once we confirm it in writing and your deposit is received.

What is included in the price?

The rate covers the yacht for the hours you book. Charters run with a licensed captain, and with crew on most yachts; captains and crew are licensed independent professionals, and your quote and charter agreement set out how they are arranged for your yacht. On most day charters, fuel, water, soft drinks, ice, towels and cups are included, and your quote confirms it. Florida sales tax, the crew gratuity (unless the yacht shows it as included), catering and extras are added on top. Your final quote shows the full amount before you pay anything.

How much is the crew gratuity?

Unless a yacht shows the gratuity as included, it is not part of the charter rate. It is customary to give the crew 15 to 20 percent of the charter price at the end of the day.

Is sales tax included?

No. Our prices are shown before Florida sales tax, which is added to your invoice.

How much is the deposit, and when is the balance due?

A deposit secures your yacht and date; the amount is shown in your quote. The balance is due 72 hours before departure, unless your booking confirmation states a different date. Holidays, special dates and multi-day charters may need a larger deposit or full payment at booking, and we tell you before you pay.

What if I need to cancel or change my date?

Cancellation and change terms depend on the yacht and the date, and they are written in your booking confirmation before you pay. As a rule, the closer you cancel to the charter date, the less can be refunded, and some holiday or special-offer bookings are non-refundable. Changes of date or yacht depend on availability and may change the price. Please ask us for the terms that apply to your yacht before you book.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Safety comes first, and the captain has the final say on whether it is safe to go out. If the captain cancels or cuts a charter short because of weather, we work with you to reschedule to another available date. Light rain or clouds usually do not stop a charter in Miami.

How many guests can we bring?

Most of our yachts take up to 13 guests, the limit set by US Coast Guard rules for private charter yachts, and each yacht shows its own maximum. Everyone on board counts, including children and babies. For a larger group we can suggest two yachts travelling together.

Where do charters depart from? Can you pick us up?

Each yacht departs from and returns to its own marina; the departure points are listed in its details, and prices can differ by departure point. Pickup from a private home or dock is sometimes possible for an extra fee. Ask us and we include it in your quote. We confirm the exact marina and meeting time with your booking.

What is the minimum charter, and what if we are late?

The minimum charter is 4 hours, and charters run between 9am and 10pm. Your time starts at the booked departure time, so please arrive about 15 minutes early: arriving late does not extend the charter. Extra time on the day depends on the yacht's availability and is charged.

Can we bring our own food and drinks?

Yes. Ready-to-eat food is best, so you enjoy the time on the water. Please leave red wine and anything in glass at home; spirits are welcome. Guests must be 21 or older to drink alcohol, and the captain may limit alcohol for safety. We can also arrange a private chef, catering, a cake or decorations for you.

Can we smoke on board?

No, smoking is not allowed on board. Vapes may be allowed at the captain's discretion, so please ask first. Illegal substances are strictly prohibited, and the captain may end the charter without refund.

Are children welcome?

Yes, children are welcome. They count towards the guest limit and must be supervised by an adult at all times. While the yacht is moving, children under 13 must wear a life jacket by law, and children's sizes are available. Let us know ages when you enquire so the crew can prepare.

Can we bring our dog?

Small pets are allowed on some yachts with a refundable security deposit: $500 on yachts up to 50 ft and $1,000 on yachts of 51 ft and more. Please ask us before booking.

Can we swim and use water toys?

Yes, on most yachts. Many include a floating mat or paddleboards, and jet skis, Seabobs, e-foils and water slides can often be added. Anyone born on or after January 1, 1988 needs a Florida boating safety ID to drive a jet ski (a free course online). Swimming stops, such as the Haulover sandbar, depend on the weather and the captain's judgement on the day.

Do you arrange trips to the Bahamas or multi-day charters?

Yes, on selected yachts. For charters of 24 hours or more, an Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA) of 35 percent of the charter price is collected in advance to cover fuel, docking, food and drinks; anything unused is returned or can go towards the crew gratuity. Every guest needs a valid passport for the Bahamas, and the crossing depends on sea conditions.

Can you help with more than the yacht?

Yes. As your concierge we can arrange luxury villas, private security, chauffeured transport, airport pick-ups, chefs, photographers, DJs and decor, so your whole stay is handled by one person.
