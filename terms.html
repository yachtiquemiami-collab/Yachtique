Booking Terms

Last updated: November 1, 2026

These terms explain how booking a yacht charter or concierge service through Yachtique works. Please read them before confirming a booking. If anything is unclear, ask us before you pay.

Our role

Yachtique is a yacht charter broker and concierge. The yachts we offer are owned and operated by independent, licensed charter operators. Charters run with a licensed captain, and with crew on most yachts; captains and crew are licensed independent professionals, and your quote and charter agreement set out how they are arranged for your yacht. Your charter is carried out under the operator's charter agreement, which you may be asked to sign before departure. If these terms and that agreement differ, the charter agreement applies to the charter itself.

Quotes and prices

Prices on our website are starting rates for the yacht and the hours shown. They can change with the date, duration, departure point and availability, and they are shown before Florida sales tax. Unless a yacht shows it as included, a gratuity of 18 percent of the charter price is added to your total. For charters of 24 hours or more, an Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA) of 35 percent of the charter price is collected in advance for fuel, docking, food and drinks, and anything unused is returned or can go towards the gratuity. Pickup from a private home or dock, water toys and other extras are charged on top. Your written quote shows the full price before you pay.

Payment

A yacht is only reserved for you once we confirm it in writing and your payment has been received. Once your yacht is confirmed available, we send you the total, with Florida sales tax included, and a secure link to pay online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Payments are processed by Stripe, and Yachtique never sees or stores your card number. Unless your quote states otherwise, the full amount is due at booking. If we agree a deposit with you instead, the balance is due 72 hours before departure, and if it is not paid on time the booking may be cancelled under the cancellation terms below.

Cancellations and changes by you

Once your charter is confirmed and paid, these refunds apply if you cancel: more than 30 days before the charter date, 80% of what you paid; 15 to 30 days before, 50%; less than 15 days before, no refund. Refunds do not include card processing or bank fees. A change of date, time or yacht depends on availability and may change the price. Please send cancellations and change requests in writing, by email or WhatsApp; the day we receive your message is the date that counts.

Weather and safety

The captain is responsible for the safety of the vessel and everyone on board and has the final decision on whether a charter departs, its route, swimming stops and whether it returns early. If your charter cannot go ahead because of unsafe weather or a technical problem with the yacht, we move it to another available date or give you a credit for what you paid, valid for 12 months from the original date. Light rain or clouds alone are not usually a reason to cancel.

Cancellations by the operator

If the operator has to cancel your charter, for example because of a mechanical problem, a safety concern or the yacht becoming unavailable, you choose between a full refund of everything you paid and a credit of the same value for a charter within 12 months. We are not responsible for other costs, such as travel or accommodation. In a verified emergency or force majeure event, such as a natural disaster, government restrictions or serious illness, exceptions can be made with supporting documents.

Guests and conduct

Most yachts take up to 13 guests, the limit for private charter yachts under US Coast Guard rules, and no yacht may exceed its stated maximum. Everyone on board counts, including children, who must be supervised by an adult; children under 13 must wear a life jacket while the yacht is moving. Guests must follow the instructions of the captain and crew at all times. Alcohol may only be consumed by guests aged 21 or older, and red wine and glass are not allowed on board. Smoking is not allowed. Small pets are allowed on some yachts only with a refundable security deposit. Illegal drugs, weapons and dangerous behavior are not allowed, and the captain may end a charter early, without refund, if guests put safety at risk or break the law. The person who makes the booking is responsible for their group, including any damage caused to the yacht beyond normal use.

Timing

The minimum charter is 4 hours, between 9am and 10pm. Please arrive at the marina about 15 minutes before departure. Charter time starts at the booked departure time, and late arrival does not extend the charter. Extending a charter on the day depends on the yacht's availability and is charged at the operator's rate.

Concierge services

Transport, security, catering and other services we arrange are provided by independent partners under their own terms, which we share with you when you book.
