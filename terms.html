Booking Terms

Last updated: October 30, 2026

These terms explain how booking a yacht charter or concierge service through Yachtique works. Please read them before confirming a booking. If anything is unclear, ask us before you pay.

Our role

Yachtique is a yacht charter broker and concierge. The yachts we offer are owned and operated by independent, licensed charter operators. Charters run with a licensed captain, and with crew on most yachts; captains and crew are licensed independent professionals, and your quote and charter agreement set out how they are arranged for your yacht. Your charter is carried out under the operator's charter agreement, which you may be asked to sign before departure. If these terms and that agreement differ, the charter agreement applies to the charter itself.

Quotes and prices

Prices on our website are starting rates for the yacht and the hours shown. They can change with the date, duration, departure point and availability, and they are shown before Florida sales tax. Unless a yacht shows it as included, the crew gratuity is not part of the rate; 15 to 20 percent of the charter price is customary. For charters of 24 hours or more, an Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA) of 35 percent of the charter price is collected in advance for fuel, docking, food and drinks, and anything unused is returned or can go towards the crew gratuity. Pickup from a private home or dock, water toys and other extras are charged on top. Your written quote shows the full price before you pay.

Deposits and payment

A yacht is only reserved for you once we confirm it in writing and your deposit has been received. The deposit amount and accepted payment methods are stated in your quote. The balance is due 72 hours before departure, unless your booking confirmation states a different date. Holidays, special dates and multi-day charters may require a larger deposit or full payment at booking; we tell you before you pay. If the balance is not paid on time, the booking may be cancelled under the cancellation terms below.

Cancellations and changes by you

Cancellation and change terms depend on the yacht and the date and are written in your booking confirmation before you pay. As a rule, the closer you cancel to the charter date, the less can be refunded, and some holiday, special-offer or multi-day bookings are non-refundable. Changes to the date, time or yacht depend on availability and may change the price. Please send cancellations and change requests in writing by email or WhatsApp.

Weather and safety

The captain is responsible for the safety of the vessel and everyone on board and has the final decision on whether a charter departs, its route, swimming stops and whether it returns early. If the captain or operator cancels because of unsafe weather or sea conditions, we work with you to move your charter to another available date, or as set out in your booking confirmation. Light rain or clouds alone are not usually a reason to cancel.

Guests and conduct

Most yachts take up to 13 guests, the limit for private charter yachts under US Coast Guard rules, and no yacht may exceed its stated maximum. Everyone on board counts, including children, who must be supervised by an adult; children under 13 must wear a life jacket while the yacht is moving. Guests must follow the instructions of the captain and crew at all times. Alcohol may only be consumed by guests aged 21 or older, and red wine and glass are not allowed on board. Smoking is not allowed. Small pets are allowed on some yachts only with a refundable security deposit. Illegal drugs, weapons and dangerous behavior are not allowed, and the captain may end a charter early, without refund, if guests put safety at risk or break the law. The person who makes the booking is responsible for their group, including any damage caused to the yacht beyond normal use.

Timing

The minimum charter is 4 hours, between 9am and 10pm. Please arrive at the marina about 15 minutes before departure. Charter time starts at the booked departure time, and late arrival does not extend the charter. Extending a charter on the day depends on the yacht's availability and is charged at the operator's rate.

Concierge services

Villas, transport, security, catering and other services we arrange are provided by independent partners under their own terms, which we share with you when you book.
