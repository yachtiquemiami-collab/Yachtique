Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Yachtique ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through yachtique.co, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, preferred date, number of guests, the yacht and services you're interested in, and any message you write. If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone, email or Instagram, we receive the information you share there.

Details sent through our enquiry form (your name, phone, email, date, number of guests and message) reach us by email and are saved in Yachtique's private Google Sheet.

Information collected automatically

To understand what visitors use, our website records anonymous counts of page views and clicks. Each count notes the page, the button or link clicked (for example WhatsApp), the yacht viewed, the language, whether the device is a phone or a computer, and the website or campaign that referred the visit. No name, email, phone number or IP address is stored with these counts. They are kept in Yachtique's private Google Sheet.

Our hosting provider, Netlify, also records basic technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and performance.

Cookies and your browser

Our website uses no cookies and no advertising or third-party tracking.

Your language choice and the yachts you save to your shortlist are stored only in your own browser (local storage). You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

How we use your information

We use the details you give us only to reply to your enquiry and to arrange your charter and any services you ask us to organize. We use the anonymous counts of page views and clicks to improve our website. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

To arrange your booking we share the necessary details with the yacht owner or charter operator, the captain and crew, and any partner you ask us to involve, such as a villa, transport, security or catering provider. We also use service providers that process information on our behalf: Netlify (website hosting and form submissions), Google (email and the private Google Sheet where we keep enquiry records and the anonymous visit counts) and Meta (WhatsApp and Instagram messages). These providers handle data under their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking details for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal or accounting obligations, then delete them. The anonymous visit counts do not identify you.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing yachtiquemiami@gmail.com. If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, you also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information online.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
