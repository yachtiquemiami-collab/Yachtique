Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Yachtique ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through yachtique.co and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, preferred date, number of guests and any message you write. Our hosting provider may also record basic technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, for security and performance.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to your enquiry, prepare charter and concierge options, arrange your booking and keep in touch about it. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

To arrange your booking we may share the necessary details with the yacht owner or operator, captain and crew, and with any partner you request (for example a villa, transport, security or catering provider). Our website is hosted by Netlify, which processes form submissions on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking details for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal or accounting obligations, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing yachtiquemiami@gmail.com.
