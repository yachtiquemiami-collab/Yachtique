For hotel and building concierges, villa and Airbnb hosts, event and wedding planners, drivers and anyone whose people ask "can you get us a boat?". You share your link, we do everything else.

How it works

  1. Sign up below. We review every request and reply within a day, usually on WhatsApp.
  2. Get your own link, like yachtique.co/?p=yourname. Share it with clients, on WhatsApp, in your welcome book or on a QR card.
  3. We take it from there. Yachts, live availability, the quote, the payment, the captain, the day itself. Your client is looked after by one broker from the first message to the dock.
  4. You earn 10% of the charter price on every booking from your link, paid by Zelle within 7 days after the charter.

Partner terms

  • Commission: 10% of the charter price the client pays for the yacht, after any discount. Not on sales tax, the service fee, gratuity or extras (food, DJ, decorations...).
  • When: paid by Zelle within 7 days after the charter takes place. Nothing is paid on a cancelled or refunded booking.
  • Which bookings: inquiries sent within 60 days of your client opening your link, from the same phone or computer. If a client tells us you sent them, we credit you too. A client who already booked or asked us directly before your link is not a referral.
  • Prices: your clients pay our normal website price. Please don't promise discounts or quote prices yourself; send them the link and we'll do the rest.
  • Fair play: no spam, no paid ads on the Yachtique name, and no booking for yourself through your own link.
  • Taxes: if you earn $600 or more in a year we'll ask for a W-9 and send you a 1099.
  • Yachtique may update these terms or end a partnership at any time; commissions already earned are always paid.
